About CYBERDUDEBIVASH SENTINEL APEX

Who we are, how our intelligence is produced, and the standards every published report is held to.

01. What this platform is

CYBERDUDEBIVASH SENTINEL APEX is an AI-powered cyber threat intelligence publication and platform operated by CyberDudeBivash Pvt. Ltd. We monitor official vulnerability authorities and global security reporting around the clock, and publish CVE analysis, exploitation alerts, ransomware and APT tracking, AI/LLM security research, and detection engineering guidance for SOC teams, security engineers, and security leaders.

The platform combines an automated intelligence pipeline — which ingests, deduplicates, scores, and cross-references public threat data — with editorial analysis and detection engineering. Subscriptions, API access, and enterprise services fund the work; see Pricing and Enterprise.

02. Editorial & sourcing standards

Every vulnerability claim we publish is expected to be traceable to a primary source:

  • The official NIST National Vulnerability Database record for the CVE
  • The CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog for exploitation claims
  • The affected vendor's own security advisory (MSRC, Fortinet PSIRT, Chrome Releases, and equivalents)
  • For AI security research: OWASP GenAI, MITRE ATLAS, and the NIST AI RMF

Severity scores are taken from the primary record, not estimated. Exploitation-in-the-wild claims require KEV listing or a vendor/government confirmation. We do not exaggerate severity for engagement.

03. Corrections policy

When a published report fails verification against primary sources, we retract it publicly: the original URL is replaced with a correction notice stating what was published, what verification found, and what the authoritative record actually says. The page is de-indexed and removed from all platform surfaces. We do not silently delete or quietly edit failed reports.

To report an accuracy issue in any published report, email bivash@cyberdudebivash.com. Corrections are reviewed against primary sources and acted on promptly.

04. How the automated pipeline works

The SENTINEL APEX pipeline continuously ingests public feeds — NVD, the CISA KEV catalog, vendor advisories, and established security press — then deduplicates, scores, and cross-references items across sources before publication. Reports generated by the pipeline carry links to their underlying sources. Automated content is aggregation and enrichment of public data; it is clearly distinct from our editorial research reports.
