    02 / Responsible Operations

    From evidence to an analyst decision.

    The SOC program is engineered around attributable evidence and authorization. Customer telemetry ingestion and verified response actions are gated until pilot certification.

    STEP 01

    Source

    Identify authorized CTI or customer data sources and preserve provenance.

    STEP 02

    Validate

    Check scope, signed source records, confidence and actual evidence bytes.

    STEP 03

    Review

    Require an authorized analyst before asserting verified detections or incidents.

    STEP 04

    Respond

    Use an agreed escalation and evidence-handling plan; no unauthorized containment.
