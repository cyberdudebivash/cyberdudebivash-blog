CTI DELIVERY ACCEPTANCE · PRODUCTION CUSTOMER GATE

Acceptance is evidence, not assumption.

This checklist defines the minimum technical evidence for a customer to treat SENTINEL APEX CTI delivery as integrated. It does not declare a customer accepted automatically.

A1
Authentication

Customer API key works only through approved headers and resolves to the intended customer tier.

CUSTOMER VERIFY
A2
Tenant isolation

Authenticated customer views and watchlists do not expose another customer's data or credentials.

CUSTOMER VERIFY
A3
Schema stability

Agreed JSON/STIX/export fields parse in the downstream integration and undocumented fields are not treated as contractual.

CUSTOMER VERIFY
A4
Evidence provenance

Source identity, references, timestamps, evidence labels, and known limitations are visible where available.

CUSTOMER VERIFY
A5
Freshness semantics

Customer understands pipeline timestamps and does not interpret current-state freshness as a guaranteed zero-delay feed.

CUSTOMER VERIFY
A6
Detection/use-case validation

Any Sigma/KQL/SIEM content is validated in the customer's environment according to its disclosed maturity and telemetry requirements.

CUSTOMER VERIFY
A7
Delivery failure handling

Retries, dead letters, webhook behavior, and customer-side endpoint failure handling are understood for enabled notification channels.

CUSTOMER VERIFY
A8
Incident escalation

Security and production-incident contacts are recorded; API keys are never sent in tickets or email.

CUSTOMER VERIFY
A9
Assurance boundary

Customer reviewer acknowledges that SOC 2-aligned operational evidence is not a SOC 2 Type I/II attestation.

CUSTOMER VERIFY
A10
Contract scope

SLA, DPA, support, audit, data-residency, and custom obligations match executed commercial terms.

CUSTOMER VERIFY
Machine-readable profile

/api/intel/cti-delivery-acceptance.json exposes the acceptance criteria for procurement/integration tooling.

Authenticated customers can export their customer-scoped assurance package from GET /api/v1/customer/assurance?download=1.

No silent acceptance.

The platform does not store or claim a customer's acceptance decision through this public page. Formal acceptance should be recorded by the customer through its own authorized process or contractual implementation record.
