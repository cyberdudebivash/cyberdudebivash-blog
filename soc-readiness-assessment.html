01 / Defined customer outcome

A practical assessment—not a dashboard demo.

Engineering findings are based on the written scope and evidence actually provided. Gaps and uncertainty are explicit; unsupported detections are never represented as verified.

Telemetry & detection coverage

Review agreed log sources, visibility, event normalization, detection objectives and relevant coverage gaps.

Investigation & evidence integrity

Examine triage workflows, timestamps, access boundaries, source traceability, retention and incident approval paths.

Prioritized improvement roadmap

Translate supported findings into owner-assigned recommendations and a staged validation plan.

02 / Deliverables and exclusions

Proposed deliverables

  • Written scope and agreed assessment criteria.
  • Evidence inventory and visibility/detection gap matrix.
  • Traceable findings with observed proof, unknowns and severity rationale.
  • Clearly marked detection-rule drafts or query recommendations where justified—not deployment-certified rules.
  • Prioritized remediation backlog and executive-ready summary.
  • Acceptance checklist and recorded customer feedback.

Explicit exclusions

  • No continuously staffed SOC, threat monitoring or guaranteed response time.
  • No autonomous host isolation, account disabling, containment or forensic seizure.
  • No unauthorized live testing, endpoint access or bulk telemetry ingestion.
  • No penetration test, SOC 2 certification, ISO certification or compliance attestation.
  • No production SIEM/EDR integration without a separately approved scope.
  • No guarantee that all vulnerabilities, threats or intrusions will be found.
03 / Controlled engagement

Acceptance is evidence, not an assumption.

  1. Enquire
    Share the business problem and general environment, without secrets.
  2. Define scope
    Confirm in-scope systems, authorization, data handling, fee and deliverables in writing.
  3. Approve access
    Agree safe evidence-transfer methods, roles and retention.
  4. Review evidence
    Carry out an authorized, bounded assessment with traceable findings.
  5. Deliver results
    Provide written observations, recommendations, limits and remediation priorities.
  6. Accept delivery
    Record customer review, agreed corrections and explicit fulfillment acceptance.
