Frequently Asked Questions

Answers on getting started, API keys, pricing, billing, and report access. Can't find what you need? Contact support directly.

Getting Started

How do I get an API key?
Register on the API Dashboard with just an email address — no card required. A Free-tier key is issued immediately, and you'll get a welcome email with your key, tier, rate limit, and links to your dashboard and the API docs.
I lost my API key — how do I get it back?
Check your inbox for the welcome email sent at registration — it includes your key. If you can't find it, contact support with the email address you registered with.
How do I authenticate API requests?
Pass your key as a bearer token or query parameter as documented on the API Docs page, which also has copy-paste request examples for every tier.

Pricing & Tiers

What are the pricing tiers?
Free ($0) — rate-limited API access and public CVE/threat pages. API Starter (₹999/$12 per month) — 5,000 calls/day, weekly intel digest. SOC Pro (₹1,499/$18 per month) — 25,000 calls/day, full IOC feed, Sigma/Yara detection rules. Sentinel Team (₹20,699/$249 per month) — 100,000 calls/day, 5 seats, STIX 2.1 and SIEM export. Enterprise Apex (from ₹82,999/$999 per month, custom-scoped) — unlimited calls, dedicated analyst, custom SLA, white-label reporting. Full feature comparison on the Pricing page.
How do I upgrade or change tiers?
Click "Upgrade" on the Pricing page or your dashboard — checkout supports Razorpay card payment or manual UPI/bank transfer. Your tier updates automatically once payment is verified.
Do prices include tax, and which currency am I billed in?
Prices are shown in INR (₹) with an approximate USD equivalent for reference. The amount charged is the INR figure shown at checkout, processed via Razorpay, UPI, or bank transfer.

Billing & Payments

Is UPI or bank transfer supported, or only cards?
Both. Card checkout runs through Razorpay. Manual UPI and bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS) are also supported — you submit your transaction reference after paying, and your tier activates once it's verified.
Can I cancel my subscription, and is there a refund policy?
Paid plans renew automatically and can be cancelled any time, effective at the end of the current billing period. If a paid service is materially unavailable due to a fault on our side, contact support within 7 days for a remedy or pro-rated refund. Full terms on the Terms page.
My payment isn't showing as verified — what do I do?
Manual UPI/bank transfers are reviewed against your submitted reference and can take some time to confirm. If it's been longer than expected, contact support with your Intent ID from the checkout confirmation.

Intelligence Reports

What kind of intelligence reports do you publish?
Hand-authored, evidence-tagged analyst reports covering active exploitation, MITRE ATT&CK mapping, detection rules (Sigma/SIEM), and threat hunting guidance — sourced from verified primary references (vendor advisories, CISA, NVD) rather than automated aggregation. Browse published reports on the Intelligence Reports page. (This is separate from the Threat Intelligence hub, which is the live, continuously-updated threat actor/campaign graph, not these analyst reports.)
How is report quality verified?
Every published report passes an internal quality gate and certification process checking structural completeness, reference verification, and evidence tagging before publication — not just written and posted.
